Amelia was born and raised in the verdant landscapes of Ireland, surrounded by a rich tapestry of plant life and an array of vibrant colors. Her father was a horticulturist and her mother an artist, their combined influences creating a perfect niche for Amelia's talents to flourish. The world of floristry became her playground, a fusion of her father's scientific knowledge and her mother's artistic insight.

At the tender age of eighteen, Amelia was invited to Munich by Duchess Luise. The Duchess, captivated by Amelia's unique floral designs, offered her a position in her estate. In the manicured gardens of the Duchess, Amelia found her calling. Her floral masterpieces became a hallmark of the estate, intertwining her youthful exuberance with the timeless elegance of the Duchy.
