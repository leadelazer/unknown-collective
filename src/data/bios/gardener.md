Carla's life began nestled in the bosom of the countryside, far from the city's crowded streets. Born to a humble farming family, her hands quickly learned the language of the earth, whispering secrets to the seeds she planted, coaxing them to life. The farm animals became her silent companions, their knowing looks exchanged in the solitude of the barnyard.

While the others in her family saw farming as mere survival, Carla saw it as a dance, a harmony of elements working together. Yet, even as her heart filled with the rhythm of the seasons, a part of her yearned for something beyond — a more refined palette, a broader canvas, a dance of beauty rather than just survival.

When the chance came for her to tend the vast gardens of the von Waldeck estate in the city, Carla took it as eagerly as a plant seeking sunlight. Her world transformed from the earthy practicality of the farm to the elaborate spectacle of noble gardens. Now she wasn't just coaxing plants to life; she was orchestrating a symphony of growth and beauty, indulging her artistic inclinations.

As she weaved her magic through the gardens, Carla emerged from her cocoon, captivating those around her with her practical wisdom and the grace she exuded. Yet, despite the flutters of admiration, she held herself with an earthy humility, her feet firmly grounded, her heart still whispering to the flowers.

In the shadow of the ornate manor, her path crossed with that of Frederike von Waldeck, the neglected daughter of the house. The unlikely friendship blossomed amidst the petals, rooted in a shared longing for connection and the mutual fascination they found in each other.
