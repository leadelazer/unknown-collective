## Talisman

The Baker's card carries the slow wisdom of someone who understands that nothing worth having can be forced. It grants patience — real patience, not the tolerance of delays, but the deep knowledge that things ripen in their own time. The bearer may find themselves more willing to wait, and more certain that the wait will mean something.

## Shadow

The shadow of the Baker is stasis mistaken for patience. When the Hanged Man's stillness becomes permanent, waiting ceases to be preparation and becomes avoidance. The bearer risks suspending the self indefinitely — deferring decisions, letting moments pass, confusing inaction with wisdom. The bread does not rise forever. At some point, it must be baked.
