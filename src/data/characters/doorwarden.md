## Talisman

The Doorwarden's card symbolises revelation, courage, and the willingness to confront what lies on the other side of a closed door. Its holder may find themselves drawn toward thresholds they have long avoided — not recklessly, but with a readiness they did not previously possess. The card does not force doors open. It makes you ready to open them yourself.

## Shadow

The shadow of the Tower warns that some doors, once opened, cannot be shut. The bearer may unleash buried truths before the ground is stable enough to receive them — triggering upheaval not from malice but from an impatience with concealment. The shadow asks: is this the right moment? And can you live with what comes next?
