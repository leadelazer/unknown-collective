## Talisman

The Brewer's card carries the energy of joy as a discipline — the understanding that celebration is not indulgence but renewal. Holders may find their capacity for pleasure returned to them: an ability to be present at the table, to receive warmth without deflecting it, and to recognise that the energy spent on life's good moments is never wasted.

## Shadow

The shadow of the Brewer is the light that becomes blinding — joy that refuses to acknowledge shadow, celebration that becomes escape. When the sun will not set, nothing rests. The bearer risks using brightness as a way to avoid what lives in the dark, and energy as a way to outpace grief. The gin should be clean. But it should not be the only thing on the table.
