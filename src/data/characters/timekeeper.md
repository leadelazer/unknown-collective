## Talisman

The Timekeeper's talisman emanates wisdom and transcendence. Holders may find themselves attuned to the subtle energies that flow through existence — perceiving hidden threads that connect all things, glimpsing the world's interconnectedness. The gift is not power over time but presence within it: an ability to see the whole arc and to move within it with grace.

## Shadow

The shadow of the Timekeeper urges caution in venturing too far into the void — the danger of becoming lost in the enigma of the unknown, drifting between worlds without grounding or purpose. The bearer risks mistaking detachment for wisdom, and observation for participation. To watch the world from too great a distance is to miss the life within it.
