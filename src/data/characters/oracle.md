## Talisman

As the holder of the Oracle's card, you are bestowed with Frederike's gift of foresight and her ability to find patterns in the chaos. Her card empowers you with a knack for anticipation and the aptitude to make decisions in a landscape of uncertainty. You may find yourself capable of uncanny insights, able to see the threads that connect the past, present, and future. You are invited to use her wisdom and vision to guide your actions. There may be times when it feels like you are on the outside looking in, just as Frederike often felt. However, remember that this unique vantage point can provide valuable perspective. Use it to see the bigger picture and steer towards the best course of action.

## Shadow

With the shadow of the Oracle, you are reminded that being an observer doesn't mean you're exempt from human connection. Do not allow your insights to isolate you from others. Instead, use your wisdom to foster understanding and empathy. Despite your ability to foresee outcomes, remember that life is a shared journey. Encourage others with your insights, but also learn to step down from your perch and engage in the world. Your journey involves not only observing and predicting but also participating in life's grand game.
