## Talisman

The talisman of the Keymaker awakens dormant knowledge. It does not grant access by force, but by recognition — you remember what you once knew, or sense what you were once meant to face. Doors do open, but only the ones that were waiting for you. Those who carry the talisman often report moments of strange clarity: rereading old letters with new eyes, sensing when someone is ready to speak.

## Shadow

The shadow of the Keymaker is not chaos, but avoidance. The bearer becomes so attuned to the potential for awakening that they begin to withhold it — sensing others' readiness but stepping aside, delaying too long, excusing inaction as mercy. They may bury truths that were meant to resurface, mistaking postponement for wisdom. Over time: a life full of unopened letters and unspoken names.
