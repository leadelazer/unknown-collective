## Talisman

The Tailor's talisman is embedded with fine threads representing the many lives he has touched. Its keeper finds a heightened sense of empathy — an uncanny ability to see beneath the surface of those around them. The card enhances intuition and grants the capacity to perceive hidden truths that lie beneath the exterior. It is a card of deep seeing, quietly offered.

## Shadow

The shadow of the Tailor cautions against losing oneself in the depths of introspection and isolation. The figure on the shadow card is hunched over their work, surrounded by darkness — consumed by an inner world at the expense of the outer. The keeper is reminded to maintain the balance between looking inward and remaining present, between witnessing others' depths and inhabiting one's own life.
