## Talisman

The Cook's card carries the energy of nourishment in its deepest sense — not merely food, but the conditions that allow things to flourish. Those who hold it may find that conflicts soften in their presence, that people stay longer and speak more honestly at their table. The gift is not charisma but warmth — a steady, patient invitation to be well.

## Shadow

The shadow of the Cook is the temptation to smooth everything over — to offer comfort where clarity is needed, warmth where honesty would serve better. When Temperance tips into appeasement, growth stalls. The bearer risks becoming so focused on keeping the peace that they prevent the confrontations that would actually resolve things.
