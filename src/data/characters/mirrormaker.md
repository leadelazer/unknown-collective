## Talisman

Valentin's obsidian mirror reflects not just physical appearance but the deepest aspects of character — the self unobscured by the pretenses one presents to the world. The card grants clarity and truth, an unerring view of what one actually is beneath the performance. This is not a comfortable gift. It is, however, a liberating one.

## Shadow

The mirror also casts a shadow of introspection — exposing the darker aspects of one's nature that one might prefer to keep hidden. Fears, insecurities, and desires that the individual is not ready to confront may surface unbidden. The shadow invites not punishment but integration: to face what the mirror shows, and to refuse to look away.
